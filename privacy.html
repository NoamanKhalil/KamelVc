Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 May 2026  ·  Effective date: 25 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kamel Ventures ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you interact with our website at kamel.vc. We are committed to handling your data in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and all applicable privacy laws.

1. Who we are (Data Controller)

Kamel Ventures is the data controller for the personal information collected through this website. For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at:

Email: hello@kamelventures.org

2. What data we collect

When you sign up through our website we collect:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Your role (investor, founder, mentor, or other) — optional
  • Intended contribution amount in USD — optional
  • IP address (recorded automatically at the time of submission)
  • Timestamp of sign-up

We do not collect any special categories of personal data (e.g. health, biometric, or financial account data) and we do not use cookies or tracking technologies on this site.

3. Why we collect it and our legal basis

We process your data for the following purposes:

  • To manage your early-access registration and keep you informed about Kamel Ventures developments — legal basis: consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), given when you tick the agreement checkbox on our form.
  • To send a confirmation email acknowledging your sign-up — legal basis: legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • To prevent duplicate registrations and protect against abuse (IP logging) — legal basis: legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. How long we keep your data

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described above, or until you request deletion. If Kamel Ventures does not proceed to launch, we will delete all sign-up data within 30 days of that decision and notify registrants by email.

5. Who we share your data with

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Your data is stored in a MySQL database hosted by Hostinger (EU servers where possible). Hostinger acts as a data processor under a Data Processing Agreement and is bound by GDPR obligations.

We will only disclose your data if required to do so by law or a competent authority.

6. International transfers

We aim to store all data within the European Economic Area (EEA). If any processing occurs outside the EEA, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses) in accordance with Chapter V of the GDPR.

7. Your rights under GDPR

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") — ask us to delete your data.
  • Right to restriction — ask us to limit how we process your data.
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Right to withdraw consent — you can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing us; this does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@kamelventures.org. We will respond within 30 days. We may ask you to verify your identity before acting on a request.

8. Right to lodge a complaint

If you believe we have not handled your data lawfully, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In the EU you can find your national authority at edpb.europa.eu.

9. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data from unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. These include encrypted HTTPS connections, access-controlled databases, and restricted admin access protected by hashed credentials. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we take our obligations seriously and will notify you of any breach as required by law.

10. Children's data

This website is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe we have done so, please contact us immediately and we will delete the data.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For material changes, we will notify registered users by email. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Questions? Email us at hello@kamelventures.org
