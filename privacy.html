Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026  ·  Effective date: May 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Kamel Ventures ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit kamel.vc and sign up for our early-access list. Kamel Ventures is a US-based non-profit initiative.

1. Who we are

Kamel Ventures is the organization responsible for the information collected through this website. For any privacy questions, contact us at:

Email: hello@kamelventures.org

2. What information we collect

When you sign up through our website we collect:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Your role (investor, founder, mentor, or other) — optional
  • Intended contribution amount in USD — optional
  • IP address (recorded automatically at the time of submission)
  • Date and time of sign-up

We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or sensitive personal data. We do not use cookies or any tracking technologies on this site.

3. How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Add you to our early-access list and keep you informed about Kamel Ventures news and updates
  • Send you a one-time confirmation email when you sign up
  • Prevent duplicate registrations and protect against abuse

We will only contact you about Kamel Ventures. We do not use your information for unrelated marketing. Every email we send includes a way to opt out.

4. How long we keep your information

We retain your information for as long as you remain on our early-access list or until you ask us to remove you. If Kamel Ventures does not proceed to launch, we will delete all sign-up data within 30 days of that decision and notify registrants by email.

5. Who we share your information with

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. Your data is stored in a database hosted by Hostinger on servers located in the United States.

We may disclose your information if required by law, a court order, or a valid government request.

6. Your choices

You can contact us at any time to:

  • Access — request a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Correct — ask us to update inaccurate information
  • Delete — ask us to remove you from our list entirely
  • Opt out — unsubscribe from our emails at any time

To make any of these requests, email us at hello@kamelventures.org. We will respond within a reasonable time, typically within 14 days.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect your information, including encrypted HTTPS connections, access-controlled databases, and admin access protected by hashed credentials. No system is completely secure, but we take the protection of your data seriously and will notify you of any breach that affects your information.

8. Children

This website is not directed at anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe we have done so, contact us and we will delete the information promptly.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will notify registered users by email.

Questions? Email us at hello@kamelventures.org
